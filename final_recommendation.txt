FINAL BUSINESS RECOMMENDATION – A/B TESTING (MARKETING)

Objective:
To evaluate whether the new marketing campaign (Test group) performs better
than the existing campaign (Control group) in terms of conversion rate and revenue.

Key Findings:
- Control Conversion Rate: 8.40%
- Test Conversion Rate: 11.53%
- Absolute Conversion Uplift: +3.13%
- p-value (Chi-square test): 1.23 × 10⁻⁸ (Statistically Significant)

Revenue Analysis:
- The Test group generated a higher average revenue per user.
- A two-sample t-test confirmed that the difference in mean revenue between
  Control and Test groups is statistically significant (p < 0.05).

Conclusion:
The Test marketing campaign significantly outperforms the Control campaign
in both user conversion and revenue generation.

Final Recommendation:
Roll out the Test campaign to all users to maximize conversions and revenue.
Continue monitoring performance metrics after full deployment.

Confidence Level:
High – conclusions are supported by strong statistical evidence
and clear business impact.
